Research architecture

Ground layer → CPCB PM₂.₅/PM₁₀, gaseous pollutants and meteorology.

Remote sensing → Sentinel-2 vegetation, MODIS MAIAC AOD and Sentinel-5P NO₂.

Controls → ERA5-Land, OSM, WorldPop, ESA WorldCover and landscape structure.

Inference → predictive ML → DML/Causal Forest → MGWR.

Chronological train/test split

Primary research question

How much urban green cover is associated with measurable reductions in PM₂.₅, and how does that relationship vary spatially after accounting for pollution, meteorological and built-environment confounders?

This dashboard is exploratory; it does not claim that NDVI causes lower PM₂.₅.
